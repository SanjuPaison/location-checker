Privacy Policy — Location Checker

This policy covers the Location Checker web widget only.

1. Birth Information You Enter

All calculations in this Widget run entirely in your own browser. Your birth date, birth time, and UTC offset are used only to compute your reading on your device for the moment you use the Widget. This information is not sent to, or stored on, any server operated by the developer.

2. Current Location

You can set a current location three ways: typing a place name and tapping "Locate" (which sends that text to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim geocoding service, a third-party provider, to convert it into coordinates), tapping "Use my current location" (which asks your browser for permission to share your device's GPS/network location directly), or selecting one of the preset city buttons (which uses fixed coordinates already built into the Widget and contacts no external service). Coordinates obtained this way are used only to compute your reading in your browser and are not stored or transmitted to the developer.

3. Third-Party Services

Geocoding requests (Section 2) are subject to Nominatim's own privacy practices; see osmfoundation.org. Device geolocation is handled entirely by your browser/operating system according to their own privacy practices and permission settings; the developer never sees this data directly.

4. This Website

This widget is designed to be embedded on lifeprediction.net, which is currently hosted using GoDaddy and built on the Nicepage platform. Like most website hosting arrangements, this may involve the automatic collection of standard technical data as a normal part of serving web pages — for example, IP address, browser type, and access times — which is handled by the hosting provider rather than actively collected by the developer. The hosting provider and platform used for this website may change over time. The widget's own code is additionally hosted on GitHub Pages.

5. Analytics

This website may use one or more third-party analytics services to measure visits and engagement. These services may collect standard technical information such as IP address, device/browser type, approximate location, and pages viewed. The specific analytics provider(s) in use may change over time; current details can be requested via the contact method below.

6. What This Policy Does Not Cover

This policy covers this web widget and the lifeprediction.net website only. It does not cover any third-party service you may be directed to (for example, OpenStreetMap, GitHub, or your device's own location services), each of which has its own separate privacy practices.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time, including to reflect changes in hosting provider, analytics provider, or the services this widget relies on. Continued use of the widget or website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
